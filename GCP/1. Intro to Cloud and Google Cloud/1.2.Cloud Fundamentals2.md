# What is a CLOUD?
- Cloud is simply “computing over the internet.”
- Instead of running apps or storing data on your own computer or your company’s servers, 
you use someone else’s servers (AWS, Azure, Google Cloud) and access them through the internet.

## What cloud actually is (the real definition)
- Cloud computing is the delivery of computing services — servers, storage, databases, networking,
software, analytics, and AI — over the internet, on demand, with pay‑as‑you‑go pricing.
## What cloud replaces
- Before cloud, companies had to:
  - Buy physical servers
  - Build data centers
  - Maintain hardware
  - Hire large IT teams
  - Handle power, cooling, networking
- Cloud removes all of this, You just request resources online, and they appear instantly.
## Key Terminology 
-  **Elasticity** -  Ability to automatically scale the resources up or down based on demand.
-  **Agility** - Ability to move fast and respond quickly to the business need.
-  **Global Reach and Geo-Distribution** - Ability to expand to the new market and deploy the application to data center quickly around the world.
-  **Availability** -  Are the application are available when users need them.
-  **Latency** - Time take to the request to travel user and server. Reduced by serving the users near by locations.
-  **Capital Expenditure (CapEX)** - Upfront cost for long-term items
-  **Operational Expenditure (OpEx)** - Routine daily costs to run the business
